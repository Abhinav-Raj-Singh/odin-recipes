Kaju Katli

Description

Kaju Katli is one of India's most popular and luxurious traditional sweets, traditionally prepared during festivals like Diwali and weddings. Made primarily from high-quality cashews, sugar, and a hint of cardamom or rose water, it features a melt-in-the-mouth texture and a delicate, elegant sheen.

Because cashews act as both the base and the binder, achieving the right sugar syrup consistency is the key secret to getting that classic, velvety bite without making it too hard or crumbly.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups raw whole cashews
  • 1 cup granulated white sugar
  • 1/3 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon pure desi ghee (plus a few extra drops for greasing)
  • 1/4 teaspoon fine cardamom powder (optional)
  • 1 to 2 sheets edible silver foil or Vark (optional)

Steps

  1. Grind the Cashews:

    1. Clean and wipe the raw cashews with a dry cloth to ensure there is no moisture.

    2. Transfer them into a dry mixer jar. Grind them in short pulses (mix-and-stop method) for 3 to 5 seconds at a time to prevent the cashews from releasing their natural oils and turning into a paste.

    3. Sift the ground cashew powder through a fine-mesh sieve to separate any coarse bits. Re-grind the remaining coarse bits and sift again until you get a fine, flour-like powder.

  2. Prepare the Sugar Syrup:

    1. In a heavy-bottomed non-stick pan, combine 1 cup of sugar and 1/3 cup of water.

    2. Turn the heat to medium and stir until the sugar completely dissolves.

    3. Let the syrup come to a boil and cook until it reaches a one-string consistency (when a drop of syrup is tested between your thumb and index finger, it forms a single unbroken string when pulled apart).
