Restaurant-Style Spicy Paneer Tikka Pizza

Description

Spicy Paneer Tikka Pizza is the ultimate fusion masterpiece, marrying the bold, smoky flavors of North Indian tandoori paneer tikka with the comforting texture of an Italian artisanal pizza.

Originating from modern Indian cafes and popular urban food hubs, this dish bridges two distinct culinary worlds by replacing traditional pizza sauce with a spiced makhani or tikka base, layered with marinated grilled paneer, and finished with fresh herbs and a fusion drizzle. It delivers a perfect balance of smoky, spicy, creamy, and cheesy notes in every single bite.

Ingredients

Pizza Base & Cheese:

  • 1 large (10 to 12-inch) pre-baked pizza crust or fresh raw pizza dough ball
  • 1.5 cups low-moisture part-skim mozzarella cheese, freshly shredded
  • 1 tbsp melted butter or garlic butter (for brushing the crust edges)
