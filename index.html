Kaju Katli

Kaju Katli features a smooth, melt-in-your-mouth
diamond-shaped fudge made from finely ground
cashews and sugar. Delicately scented with cardamom
and often garnished with edible silver leaf,it is a
quintessential Indian festival treat.
